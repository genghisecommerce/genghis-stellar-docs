## Where the company operates

- Registered and headquartered in London. The team works remotely from Europe, India and Nigeria.
- The catalogue serves 86 country markets (page 4). Customers bought from 46 countries by home market, counted to 19 October 2026 (page 2).
- Customers pay in crypto through a licensed third-party payment processor. Genghis does not run a custody service for customers.
- Genghis applies purchase limits. Without an account: $500 in 24 hours and $2,000 in 30 days. With a free account: $5,000 in 24 hours and $10,000 in 30 days. There is no identity check to buy below those limits. Limits live since 25 September 2026.
