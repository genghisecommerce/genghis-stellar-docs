# 11. Partnerships and suppliers

Genghis resells digital goods from third-party suppliers and takes payment through a licensed processor. This page states what is public and describes the rest without names or terms. Status: LIVE.

## What is described without names

Supplier names, prices and contract terms are commercial information and are not published.

| Type | What Genghis buys | How |
|---|---|---|
| Global gift card and voucher supplier | Gift cards and vouchers across many country markets | Direct API integration, codes returned by the system |
| Game key marketplace | Game keys | Direct API integration |
| eSIM provider | eSIM plans for around 50 countries and Europe | Direct API integration, delivery in about 8 seconds |
| Mobile top-up and payment voucher suppliers | Top-ups and e-wallet vouchers | Direct API integration |

How suppliers are handled, in public terms:

- Genghis sells as principal. It buys the code from the supplier after the customer pays, and prices at a margin of a few percent. There is no platform fee on top of the price.
- Prices are updated twice a day from live exchange rates. Nothing is sold below cost.
- Suppliers do not endorse Genghis, and Genghis does not imply a partnership with any brand it sells. A brand's gift card on Genghis is a purchase, not a partnership.

## Stellar wallets

The wallets in the proof of concept (Freighter, xBull, Albedo, Hana) are used through the open-source Stellar Wallets Kit. The proof of concept does not rely on any agreement with a wallet provider, anchor or exchange.
