The Crypto Livability Index ranks countries by how workable it is to live on crypto: access, regulation, spending rails, infrastructure and community. Two rankings are published: a rails ranking and a livability ranking that reweights for how much people in each country need crypto. The method and all scores are in the data set.

Why it matters for this application: the report is original research from the team that runs the marketplace. It gives Genghis a citable data set on where crypto can actually be spent, including gift card availability by country, which is the product this grant builds a payment path for.
