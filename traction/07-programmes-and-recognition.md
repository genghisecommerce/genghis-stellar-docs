## The Algorand co-marketing campaign

A two-week campaign in December 2025 with Algorand, aimed at people who already hold USDC on Algorand. Measured on WooCommerce orders:

| Metric | Value | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Orders | 274 | 15 to 22 December 2025 |
| Sales | $4,995 | Same |
| Buyers | 240 | Same |
| Average order value | $18.23 | Same |
| Buyers who came back in the following month | 3 | January 2026 |
| Lifetime sales per buyer | $26.89 | To 19 October 2026 |

What it shows, plainly:

- The campaign brought many first-time buyers quickly, paying in USDC on Algorand.
- Those buyers spent little and few returned. 326 of the 1,395 payments in the [payment evidence](https://github.com/genghisecommerce/genghis-payment-evidence) settled in USDC on Algorand.
- Campaign orders are excluded from retail sales (page 2) so that one promotion does not look like organic demand.
- Acquisition has been organic since January 2026.
