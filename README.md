# Genghis on Stellar

Public documentation for the Genghis Stellar Community Fund submission.

- [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md): the technical architecture, version 3 (9 November 2026). Every order settles in USDC on a Genghis Stellar account, from a Stellar wallet, an embedded wallet or app, an AI agent through standard x402, or another chain through CCTP, Near Intents or Axelar.
- [MILESTONES.md](MILESTONES.md): the 10 deliverables, dates and budget ($88,000 in three tranches), and the tranche 3 on-chain metric.
- [Traction and supporting evidence](traction/README.md): every figure in our application, with its definition, period and source.
- [genghis-stellar-poc](https://github.com/genghisecommerce/genghis-stellar-poc): the testnet checkout demo (USDC first, live Genghis products), live at https://genghisecommerce.github.io/genghis-stellar-poc/
- [genghis-mcp-readonly](https://github.com/genghisecommerce/genghis-mcp-readonly): the read-only agent discovery server this design builds on.
- [Demo video](https://youtu.be/AHclPxdSYRs): 2 minutes 38, the live site, the testnet checkout and what the Build award adds (November 2026).
- [genghis.pro](https://www.genghis.pro): the live marketplace.

Genghis is a live marketplace for digital goods, trading since October 2025: gift cards, game keys, eSIMs and mobile top-ups, paid in crypto.
